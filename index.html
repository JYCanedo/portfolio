About

I graduated from the University of North Texas in December 2024 with a B.A. in Media Arts, and I've spent the years since in live control rooms and on short-film sets learning what it actually takes to get a production across the finish line — on air, on schedule, on budget.

That range is the point: I've called shots from behind a switcher, mixed audio live, and run a camera under broadcast pressure, and I've also managed the schedule, budget, and crew on independent film sets. I'm based in Houston, TX and open to relocating for the right production, broadcast, or operations role.

Production Management

Production Manager

Tastebuds

Tastebuds is a UNT Media Arts short directed by Lola Garza. I was Production Manager on three shoot days plus a pickup, two houses and a studio in Denton, TX. My job was keeping the budget, equipment, and paperwork moving for a 38-person crew across ten departments: purchase orders, a running budget that closed at $528, call sheets, the works. Every day wrapped on schedule, and the film premiered that May.

Production Coordinator

Recurve

Recurve was a three-day shoot in March, directed by Santiago Cruz, across four locations: a ranch, a university building, a hotel room, a Walmart. Day one didn't go to plan: rain, a noisy set, and equipment that showed up late pushed the start back and made reshoots look likely. We still wrapped on schedule that day, and I put together the equipment breakdown for a proper reshoot the following week.
